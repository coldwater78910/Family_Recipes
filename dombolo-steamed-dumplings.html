Dombolo (Steamed Dumplings)

Prep: 15 mins Cook: 20 mins Total: 35 mins
Medium Traditional
breaddumplingsteamed

Soft steamed dumplings, perfect placed on top of stew or mince to soak up all the flavour.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups - flour
  • 50ml - oil/butter
  • 1 Tblsp - Baking Powder
  • 2 tsp - salt
  • 1/4 cup - sugar
  • 1 cup - water, or enough to make a soft sticky dough

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients together in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add 1 cup of water, or enough that the mixture becomes a soft, kind of sticky mass of dough when kneaded. Let it rise for up to an hour, reshape into small balls, then let it rise again.
  3. Place the balls on top of a stew or mince in a pot with the lid on.
  4. Cook slowly on a low heat for about 20 minutes until the dombolo are cooked and puffed up. They should be steamed and full of flavour.
